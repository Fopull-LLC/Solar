# Solar UI style: "Flight Hardware"

Solar's interface is built like the equipment in the game: enamel-painted console
plates with screwed bezels, keycaps that press in, CRT displays where live numbers
glow, and labels printed or stencilled onto the metal. It should look like it was
issued with the spacecraft, not drawn over it. It belongs to a grounded,
NASA/ESA-era world rendered with PSX restraint. The PSX feel comes from a limited,
warm palette, plain industrial type and modest-resolution material. It never comes
from big pixels, dithering or chunky noise.

This file is the reference for anyone adding a screen, and for the base's
signage, which uses the same type and palette.

- Tokens: `ui/solar.tokens.ron`
- Styles: `ui/solar.uistyle.ron` (every style named here is defined there)
- Frames: `textures/ui/plates.png` and `textures/ui/hardware.png`, generated by
  `tools/gen_ui_textures.py`
- Fonts: `fonts/`, built by `tools/build_ui_font.py`. Licences are in
  `fonts/README.md`.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Physical before digital.** Every surface is a material: enamel plate, CRT
   glass, a keycap or a riveted placard. If you can't say what something is made
   of, it isn't finished.
2. **The light comes from above.** Plates are a touch lighter at the top. Bevels
   are lit on the top and left edges and shaded on the bottom and right. Text
   carries a 1 px drop shadow, as if printed on a raised surface.
3. **One accent.** Signal orange marks what is on, selected or next. Everything
   else is neutral enamel and ink, and state colours mean state.
4. **Numbers live on screens.** The green phosphor monospace is only for live
   values inside a CRT display (the flight instruments, the device console, the
   save-slot listing, the seed field). In sentences, on cards and on chips,
   numbers use the label face in ink.
5. **Material, not pattern.** Surface texture is low contrast and soft: film
   grain at one physical pixel, plus gentle mottling of the paint. You should feel
   it, not see it.

## 3. Type

| Face | File (token) | From | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solar UI | `SolarUI.ttf` (`ui`, project `ui_font`) | Barlow Semi Condensed Medium | sentences, body, descriptions |
| Solar Label | `SolarLabel.ttf` (`label`) | Barlow Semi Condensed SemiBold | printed capitals, keys, buttons, numbers on plates, map tags |
| Solar Mono | `SolarMono.ttf` (`mono`) | Share Tech Mono | live numbers on CRT displays only |
| Solar Stencil | `SolarStencil.ttf` (`stencil`) | Saira Stencil One | the SOLAR title, panel names, warning placards, signage |

Barlow is a DIN-like grotesk drawn after highway and aviation signage. It reads
as a flight manual, and the semi-condensed cut fits more text than Roboto did in
the same width. Share Tech Mono is a terminal-era character generator: every digit
is the same width, so a number that ticks over holds still. Saira Stencil One is
the cut-through stencil that real hardware and buildings are marked with. Use it
large, never for sentences.

**Scale** (design units at 720 lines): `micro` 11, `caption` 13.5, `body` 15.5,
`title` 20, and for numbers on displays `readout` 21 and `display` 28. Panel
headings are stencil at 17 with 2.2 tracking. The main title is stencil at 84.

- **Labels** are capitals in `label`, `muted`, with tracking 1.5–2.5. They are
  printed on the plate (`panel/label`, `hud/label`, `hud/chipLabel`).
- **Running text** is sentence case in `ui` (`panel/body`, `hud/hint`).
- **Buttons** are `label`. Menu buttons are capitals tracked 3. Panel buttons
  are title case tracked 0.4–0.6.
- **Numbers on a plate** (balance, price, reward, chip values) use `label` in ink.
  **Numbers on a screen** use `mono` in `phos`.

### Signage (for the base and facilities)

Building and pad markings use the same kit, so a screenshot reads as one world:

- **Building identifiers** (`VAB`, `OPS`, `PAD 1`) are **Saira Stencil One** in
  capitals, tracking about 0.1 em. Paint them in `ink` (0.92 0.89 0.81) on dark
  enamel or concrete, or in black on safety yellow (`warn`) or signal orange
  (`accent`).
- **Placards and secondary signs** (`COMMAND CENTRE`, `NO ENTRY`, load ratings)
  are **Barlow Semi Condensed SemiBold** capitals, tracking about 0.08 em,
  usually in a ruled box or on a riveted plate.
- Hazard stripes are black with `warn` at 45°. Use them only where something is
  actually dangerous.
- Textures should be modest in resolution and sampled smoothly, with paint wear
  that is soft and low contrast. Avoid pixel-art lettering and saturated blue
  signage.

## 4. Materials and frames

Every container is a flat enamel fill with three layers on top. First, a
light-from-above gradient. Second, the engine's grain at one physical pixel
(`grain: (amount: 0.003, scale: 0.5)`). Third, a frame sprite tinted by
`border_color`. A frame's art uses only two inks. White becomes the bezel paint,
or orange on hover. Black stays black and gives depth.

| Cell | Sheet | Corner | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `plate` (four cross-head screws) | plates.png | 10 | panels, menu cards (`panel/frame`, `menu/card`) |
| `plateBare` | plates.png | 6 | the objectives card (`hud/card`) |
| `screen` (enamel bezel and CRT glass) | plates.png | 8 | flight instruments, device console (`hud/instrument`) |
| `plateBare` (small) | hardware.png | 6 | rows, chips, stage cards |
| `key` / `keyDown` | hardware.png | 6 | every button: raised, then pressed or latched |
| `well` | hardware.png | 6 | save slots, text fields |
| `tag` (rivets) | hardware.png | 8 | walk-up prompt, toast, warning caption |

The big cells' middles carry a soft worn-enamel mottling: two octaves of blurred
noise and a few rubbed scuffs. It is drawn as one ink at a gently varying alpha
and dithered on export, so it never forms contour steps. An element smaller than
twice its corner (meters, tracks, pips, lamps) uses a plain 1 px `border` instead
of a frame.

## 5. Components

| Component | Style(s) | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Panel | `panel/frame` over `panel/scrim` | screwed enamel plate, opaque, tight drop shadow |
| Panel name | `panel/heading` | stencil, ink, 1 px shadow |
| Glance card | `hud/card` | bare enamel plate, 93% opaque |
| Primary button | `panel/action`, `menu/primary` | orange enamel key with a dark printed legend. Pressing it swaps to the pressed frame and darkens the face. |
| Secondary button | `panel/secondary`, `menu/secondary`, `hud/button` | grey enamel key with a soft legend. On hover the rim lights orange and the legend turns ink. |
| Danger button | `panel/danger`, `menu/delete` | dark key with a red legend. When armed (`selected`) the key turns red. |
| Segmented choice / toggle / tab | `panel/choice` | a row of keys. The one in force is latched in, with an orange rim and legend. |
| Slider | `ctl/track`, `ctl/fill`, `ctl/handle`, `ctl/value` | a fader: inset groove, orange enamel strip, light cap, value in label ink |
| List row | `panel/row`, `panel/rowActive` | a flat strip that lifts into a key on hover. `selected` is a warm wash with an orange rim. Rows don't set a text colour, because tier and affordability tints are the row's own. |
| Tooltip | `hud/tooltip` | a light enamel tag with ink text and a black edge |
| Status chip | `hud/chip`, `hud/chipWarn` | small plate with a printed label and an ink value. Caution is a yellow placard. |
| Gauge | inline in `status_hud` / `inventory_panel` | a dark groove with an ink bar that turns caution yellow when full |
| HUD readout | `hud/instrument`, `hud/value*`, `hud/micro` | a CRT display in a bezel: phosphor numbers, dim-phosphor legends |
| Annunciator | `hud/lamp*` | a lens, dark until true. Green advisory, yellow caution, red warning. |
| CRT line | `crt/row*` | a switchable line on a console screen: lit phosphor, caution while moving, dim when unavailable |
| Prompt / toast / caption | `hud/prompt`, `hud/toast`, `hud/caption*` | riveted placards. The caption title is stencil red. |
| Map and world tags | `orbit_map.lua` label | enamel tag with a dark edge and the label face in ink |
| Main menu | `menu/*` | stencilled SOLAR, orange maker's mark, save slots as CRT listings |

## 6. Iconography

Use the glyphs the type kit carries (merged from DejaVu): ▸ ◂ › for direction,
✓ ✕ for done and remove, ○ ● for open and done goals, ◆ ★ for the contract on
the books and the programme, and the SAS set ◎ ◆ ⊕ ⊗ △ ▽ ⇡ ⇣ ⊡. Icons are text,
so they take the text colour of their style. Don't use emoji, multicolour icons or
outline-icon sets.

## 7. Motion

Hardware clicks; it doesn't bounce. Keys transition in 50–60 ms with `OutQuad`,
and pressing one swaps the frame instantly. Never use `OutBack` or `OutElastic`,
glows or pulsing. Static pieces snap (`duration: 0`). Reduced motion is the
engine's and already stills transitions.

## 8. Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Put a panel in a frame and let the plate carry it | Draw a rounded rectangle with a 1 px teal outline |
| Use one orange thing per screen that says "this one" | Spend the accent on decoration, headings or edges |
| Put live numbers on a screen in phosphor mono | Set a sentence or a price on a card in monospace |
| Set labels as printed capitals in `label` | Write Title Case labels in the body face |
| Keep surface grain at amount ≤ 0.003, scale 0.5 | Use blocky grain (scale ≥ 1) or visible dither |
| Use a frame only above twice its corner size | Frame a 6 px meter (the frame smears) |
| Name a font with the token (`font: "label"`) | Hard-code a hex colour in Lua. Add a style instead. |

## 9. Engine limits found while building this (floptle cards)

1. **A font that only a style names is never loaded.** `ensure_ui_fonts`
   (`crates/floptle-editor/src/ui_game.rs`, around line 413) registers only fonts
   named on ECS `ElementSpec`s and in script texts. Styles are applied to Node
   copies (`style_layer`, around line 633), so `font: "mono"` in a `.uistyle.ron`
   falls back silently to `ui_font`. That is why the old `mono` (Noto Sans Mono)
   never showed. Repro: a token `fonts: {"x": "fonts/Any.ttf"}`, a style with
   `font: "x"`, and a text element with that style; the text draws in the project
   font. Workaround: a hidden "font primer" element per face in `status_hud.lua`,
   `menu_settings.lua` and `catalog_panel.lua`. Remove it once the engine
   registers style and token fonts.
2. **The UI sampler is always linear** (`crates/floptle-render/src/ui.rs`, around
   line 685, `ui-sampler`). There is no per-texture or per-style nearest or
   pixelated option, and no mipmaps, so a large 9-slice middle minified more than
   about 2× aliases. The art is therefore kept low-frequency.
3. **Grain is added in linear space** (`ui.wgsl`, around line 284: `col.rgb +
   n * amount`). The same `amount` is invisible on a light fill and very strong on
   a dark one, and its cells are square (`floor(px / cell)`), so any `scale` above
   about 0.67 shows blocks at 1080p. A perceptual (sRGB-space) amount and a
   smooth or blue-noise option would make it usable as material.
4. **A 9-slice frame cannot set a different texel density from the UI scale.**
   Corners are drawn at texture pixels × layer scale (`nine_slice`, around line
   193), so a frame can't use higher-resolution art at the same on-screen corner
   size. A `slice_scale` or "pixels per unit" field would allow crisp 2× art.
5. **A style cannot set an image or a second frame layer**, so a surface texture
   has to ride in the frame's middle patch and stretch with the panel. A
   `surface: (texture, tiling)` style property, tiled rather than stretched,
   would allow real paper or enamel textures.
