astronaut_male.glb / astronaut_female.glb — the crew's EVA suit
helmet_*.glb / visor_*.glb / lamps_*.glb — its helmet, gold visor and lamps
Written by tools/gen_suit.py, which models the geometry in Blender through
tools/gen_suit_blender.py (regenerate there; don't edit the files).

THE SUIT (the mesh, the head under the helmet, its skin weights, and
textures/suit/*.png)
New geometry built by tools/gen_suit.py / tools/gen_suit_blender.py on the rig
above (no vertex of the original body is used), for Solar. Because each suit
file carries the CC-BY-SA rig, the files as a whole remain CC-BY-SA
(share-alike), with attribution to Clint Bellanger for the rig. The helmet,
visor and lamps files carry no rig, but are modelled in the rig's space for
the suit, and are released under the same CC-BY-SA terms with the suit. The
textures are procedural (tools/gen_suit.py); the agency emblem comes from
tools/base_tex.py; lettering is set in the project's own fonts
(fonts/README.md, SIL OFL).

THE FACES (faces/head_*.glb, faces/hair_*.glb, faces/cap.glb, textures/faces/*)
The heads are built on the realistic head (animation topology) from Blender
Studio's "Human Base Meshes" bundle v1.4.1 (CC0, blender.org/download/demo-files),
kept as tools/faces_base/head_base.npz; tools/gen_faces.py deforms it into
eight structures and adds the face rig, weights, eyes, teeth, textures, hair
cards and the comm cap (new work, CC0). The heads hang on the suit's Head bone
(scripts/face.lua); the suit itself keeps only the neck.
