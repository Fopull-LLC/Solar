# Long-session soak, 2026-11-03 (engine 0.117.1-dev.1385.926b81d)

The player's report: a long session gets slower, and going to the menu and back
helps a little. tools/perf/soakprobe.sh, 2 segments of 840 s each (one locked
`floptle play` per segment; segment 1 CONTINUEs segment 0's save). Each cycle:
a walk and 6 laser bites on new ground, the panels, time warp, a crew switch, a
flight from the VAB or a rover drive (alternating), reverted to the builder and
out of the VAB, a save; a MAIN MENU round trip and CONTINUE every third cycle.
Per segment: 11–12 cycles, 3 menu round trips, about 6 launches, 5 rover drives,
11 saves. A census every 60 s. Both runs used the same frozen copy of the tree
(work/soak1102/snap; the after run is that copy plus this pass's six hunks only).
Load: before 4.2–5.4, after 4.0–5.9.

## What grows

Everything a script could reach, plus the engine's counts, stays flat within a
segment and across the menu round trips:
- Lua memory after a full collect: 15–19 MB.
- Nodes: about 1,000 (the census's tree walk agrees with perf.counts within 50).
- Script instances: 93–97.
- Rigidbodies: 237–250.
- Craft, props, decals and draws: no trend.
- Terrain memory: 480–760 MB, following where the crew stands.
- Each script's ms/frame: every least-squares slope is under ±0.025 ms per 5 minutes.
- Script global state: only bounded tables change (system_generator.GENSPEC
  grows once, when the first warp visits the other systems; weather_map.last_sys,
  sfx.played and flora_field.obstacles go up and down).

**The one thing that grows without bound** is GPU resources that scripts made
and the engine keeps. The engine holds `mesh.build` meshes and
`assets.textureFrom*` / `camera.captureTexture` textures until `mesh.free` /
`assets.release` or Stop. A scene load does not free them, and the engine
publishes no count of them. The probe wraps those calls to count what is live
and who made it.

| live after each menu round trip (before) | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
|---|---|---|---|
| meshes: climate (lakes, rivers) | 4 | 10 | 16 |
| meshes: base_facilities (paving, signs) | 0 | 5 | 10 |
| meshes: flora_field beam, flora_gen empty LOD | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| textures: catalog_panel part icons (via build_host) | 11 | 21 | 33 |
| textures: builder_crew portraits (via build_host) | 1 | 2 | 4 |
| textures: emblem (AgencyLib) | 0 | 2 | 4 |

The menu "helps" because it ends the scene: Lua state, nodes and bodies start
again. Everything in this table, though, stays behind and grows with every load.

## Fixed

Each owner now gives back what it made when its scene goes (`onDestroy`). A
replaced icon or portrait is released at once.
- build_host.lua: the part icons and crew portraits.
- climate.lua: `clear_water`.
- base_facilities.lua: the paving meshes.
- emblem.lua: the live emblem, the editor preview and the slot cards' small emblems.
- flora_field.lua: the rare-plant beam.
- flora_gen.lua: the empty LOD mesh.

costs GPU memory, not frame time, at this length. On the player's 8 GB card
with a 256 MiB BAR1 window it is the kind of growth that ends in an NVRM
VA-space failure after enough loads.

## Not reached / left

- **Module-local tables are invisible to the probe.** Luau has no
  `debug.getupvalue`, so the probe cannot size them. The flat Lua live memory
  bounds them all: 15–19 MB over 28 windows.
- **builder.lua's fairing preview shells** (`shells`) are freed when replaced,
  but builder.lua has no `onDestroy`. Each VAB visit with a fairing therefore
  leaves its shell meshes behind. The fix is a patch to the builder owner, not
  applied here: `work/soak1102/builder_shells_onDestroy.patch`.
- **builder_proc's mesh cache** (drawn members and panels) has the same gap.
  It is shared between the world's and the overlay's instances, and launched
  parts may draw the overlay's meshes, so it needs a deliberate owner before
  anything frees it.
- **The part icons are photographed again after every load** (now released,
  not leaked). Keeping them across loads would need state that survives the
  scene.
