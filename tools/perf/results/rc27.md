## Engine issues found (framed generally)

1. **A held force or torque restarts the sleep count however small, even when the body's
   support cancels it.** A 1,002-part compound with |v| 0.000 for 30 s reads `wake force`
   every frame and never sleeps while a script applies its per-tick force/torque (Solar's
   piloted vessel_controller; not its reaction wheels or SAS — likely the aero or water
   tick's force at rest); the moment the script stops it sleeps
   within 2.7 s (physics 48 → 0.8 ms/frame). `Compound::apply_force/apply_torque`
   (compound.rs ~395–412) wake on any non-zero vector. Ask: judge stillness by motion
   alone (the rc27 whole-count rule already does); a force that leaves the body inside
   its stillness budget should not reset the count, or let a force be applied
   "without waking". Any game with wind, buoyancy or thrusters idling on parked bodies
   hits this.
2. **`wake` latches the first cause and never names the current waker.** `WakeReason::or_own`
   keeps any non-`settling` cause when the body's own motion breaks the count, so a rover
   released by a joint reads `joint` on 3,444 of 3,444 frames while something else (only
   present while a script pilots it) keeps it awake. Ask: report the cause of the most
   recent count reset (or both: what started it and what last reset it).
3. **A resting 1,002-part compound kept awake costs ~70 % more physics on rc27:**
   48.4 / 52.6 / 48.6 ms/frame (static 46–50) in three rc27 runs vs 28.0–29.0 in three
