# Solar on Floptle 0.117.0-rc37 — trial against rc33 (2026-10-26, 16:17–17:30)

Solar HEAD a2ee5ef (already pinned to rc37). Every run was silent (ALSA null), on
XWayland with vsync Off, one engine at a time under the shared lock. `load` is
`uptime`'s 1-minute average. The engine developer was compiling the whole time, so
the load ran 5–40, and was mostly 6–14 during the benches. rc33 and rc37 were run
back to back for the comparison in section 4.

**Probe-only patches (scratch copies only, never Solar):**
- `funds.patch` puts back the $99,999 testing balance on the system scene's
  company node. It is the reverse of 16afba7. On the real $500 the stock starter
  (about $750) cannot be afforded after the flag and beacon, so the demo's LAUNCH
  does nothing. The demo and the gameplay smoke used it; rc33's runs had that
  balance in the scene.
- For the fog runs, `graphics.lua` has `autoLower = false`. Without it, the load
  made the game drop Ultra to High.

## For E (framed generally)

1. **The sun shadow pass costs 2–6× more GPU since rc33 on a moving planet with
   ground edits or a moving vehicle**, with fog shafts from the map off.
   - Repro: `bench.sh 0.117.0-rc33 base rover`, then `bench.sh 0.117.0-rc37 base
     rover`, and compare the `sun shadow map` pass in b_dig_meadow and e_rover.
   - Suspects:
     - rc36 `render_frame.rs`, which moved the sun pass ahead of the prepass
       (maybe a lost cache, or the map is redrawn each frame)
     - rc37 `space.rs` (6e755994), whose carried statics may now count as moved
       casters every tick
2. **Physics static and sweep time on a planet on rails roughly doubles since rc37.**
   - Any game with a vehicle, a rocket or ground edits on a moving planet pays it.
   - Suspect: `Editor::carried_statics` in `crates/floptle-editor/src/space.rs`. Its
     cache is invalidated by every spawn, despawn or collider change, which happens
     constantly while digging (debris) or driving. Moving every carried static each
     tick may also wake the broadphase.
   - Repro: the same benches, comparing the physicsStatic and physicsSweeps parts.
3. (Minor) The "stored and never read" param warning (host.rs:8506) fires for params
   a script reads through `params.x` without a `defaults` entry. It should say "not
   declared" rather than "never read", or count a read as a declaration.
