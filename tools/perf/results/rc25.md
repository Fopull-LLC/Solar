Forced sleep (`node.asleep = true` at t = 10 s): the rover is awake one frame
later with wake `joint`; the big craft is awake one frame later with wake
`settling`. So on rc25 the frame ride is exact (drift 0.000 m) and simple bodies
sleep, but: (a) the braked rover's wheel joints wake it every step (joints.rs
impulse > 1e-6 m → `wake(Joint)`); Solar brakes with `motor = 1, motorSpeed = 0`,
which `drives()` does not count, so it is the correction itself; (b) the
1,002-part compound is woken by something that the settle pass then overwrites
with `settling` (world.rs step_compound sets `wake = Settling` whenever the step
was slow), so the readout never names it; (c) a resting box reads `moving` at
\|v\| 0.01 m/s.
